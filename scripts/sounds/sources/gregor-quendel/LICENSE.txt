The files in this folder are unmodified copies of the pack's MP3 files that scripts/sounds/recipes.mjs uses.
The sounds the app plays (web/public/sounds/*.mp3) are modified: trimmed, pitch-shifted, layered, levelled
and looped by scripts/sounds/build.mjs. Credits: web/public/sounds/LICENSES.md and the sound settings in the app.
